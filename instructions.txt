HOW TO TAKE YOUR HANDWRITING PHOTO

Writing the sample
1. Use plain white paper. Avoid lined, squared or coloured paper; the
   printed lines can be mistaken for writing. A4 is the default size.
   If your paper is a different size, enter its real width and height
   in millimetres.
2. Use a dark pen (black or dark blue). A pencil is fine if it's dark.
   Avoid light pencil, coloured or metallic ink, and felt pens that bleed.
3. Write this sentence, exactly as shown:
   The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
4. Write in print (separate letters), not joined-up cursive. Leave
   small gaps between letters and clear gaps between words.
5. Write at your normal size and in your normal style. Don't try to be
   extra neat; the app is measuring how you usually write.
6. Leave a clear margin of blank paper around the writing, and don't
   add anything else to the page (no drawings, names or crossings-out).

Setting up
7. Put the paper flat on a table. Smooth out folds and curled corners.
8. Choose a table that is darker than the paper (wood or a dark cloth
   works well), so the app can find the edges of the page.
9. Find even, bright light, such as daylight from a window or a room
   with ceiling lights on. Don't let your phone, hand or head cast a
   shadow on the page.
10. Turn the flash off. It causes glare and bright spots.

Taking the photo
11. Hold the phone directly above the page, screen parallel to the
    paper, not at an angle.
12. Fit the whole page in the photo with a little table showing on all
    four sides. Don't cut off any corner.
13. The page should fill most of the photo. Move closer rather than
    using zoom.
14. Tap the writing on screen to focus, then hold the phone still and
    take the photo.
15. Check the photo before sending: the writing should be sharp when
    you zoom in, with no blur, shadows or glare.
16. Send the original photo. Don't crop, filter, edit or add stickers.
    JPEG or PNG works best. On an iPhone, if the upload fails, go to
    Settings > Camera > Formats and choose "Most Compatible".

If the app asks for a better photo
- "Blurry": hold the phone steadier, or rest your elbows on the table.
- "Too faint": use a darker pen or brighter light.
- "Not enough writing": make sure the whole sentence is in the photo.
- "Couldn't separate the letters": write in print with small gaps
  between letters.
- "Page edges weren't found": use a darker table and show all four
  corners, or drag the corners into place on the preview screen.

Your privacy
- Your photo is analysed and then discarded; the app does not store it.
- If you're giving a sample for the team's research, you'll be asked
  for permission first, and your handwriting won't be made public.
