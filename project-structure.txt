Real-photo findings (data/, 5 photos)
  - None followed the photo instructions (no page edges visible), so mm sizes were inflated and every
    score was "Hard to read" (35-48): artefacts of the photos, not the handwriting.
  - skibidi.jpeg is rotated 90 degrees; deskew only handles +/-15 degrees, so it measured sideways text.
